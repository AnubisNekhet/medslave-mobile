## Reels and feed filters

- With a feed filter on, reels show only that filter's cards.
- Picking a deck that isn't in the filter tells you so instead of opening it.
- A deck you pick plays on its own until you leave Reels; next time, Reels starts on the card you were on, then continues with the whole filter.
- Progress shows the filter's name under the ring and counts only its cards.
